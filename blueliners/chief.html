Bottom line · read this if nothing else

I help officers buy fixer-uppers and farmhouses, then I put them in the seat of a tractor and teach them to run it, so they can do the demolition on those rehabs themselves: driveways, fences, sheds, gardens, pools. The demo work becomes paid, on-the-job training, and over time it becomes a contracting business the officer owns.

It is built for officers stepping away from active duty: on leave, injured, nearing retirement, or retired.

Narrative

WHY POLICE OFFICERS
  1. 01Officers showed up at the turning points of my working life. A downtown Orlando officer looked out for me on bike-taxi shifts. A retired officer brought me into tree work.
  2. 02Officers already have the one thing this trade can't teach: credibility. Homeowners trust them on the property.
  3. 03"Blueliners" means the opposite of redlining an engine. We run low and slow, all torque and no strain, one job at a time, so officers can regain their peace of mind.
  4. 04Cops serving cops: the program is designed so about 90% of customers are officers and their families.

Safeguards

AGENCY-FRIENDLY
  • Officers follow their agency's outside-employment policy and any leave or benefit rules. Nothing starts until they've cleared it.
  • No badges, uniforms or agency names on any job.
  • Written safety program and protective gear on every job.
  • Body cameras on every job, for teaching only. The officer owns the footage. It is shown only to prospective Blueliners and kept off the internet.
  • Supports peer support, EAP and counseling. Never replaces them.
  • Board of 5 to 7 seats, majority independent, no family seats. The board sets apprentice pay.
  • My real estate and insurance services are always optional, disclosed to the board in writing, and I step out of related votes.

Real Estate · Officer Fixer-Upper Pipeline

FL SALES ASSOCIATE · FARM & RANCH
Find it

I work as the officer's buyer's agent on farmhouses and fixer-uppers in neighborhoods that need them.

Price it right

Each one is picked with room for an addition or an accessory dwelling unit (ADU).

Finance it

Purchase, renovation, farm or construction loans. I help line up the lender.

Demo it

I teach the officer to run the tractor, and they do the tear-out with a Blueliners crew. That's the training, and it saves money on the rehab.

Build & insure it

Licensed trades finish the build. I write the homeowner's policy, and later the truck and business coverage.

Each house is a hands-on classroom. The officer leaves with a home, real equity from their own labor, and the skills to do it again for other people.
